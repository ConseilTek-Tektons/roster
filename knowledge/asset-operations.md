# Roster asset-intelligence guide

## Discovery and normalization

Preserve the raw source observation before normalization. Match publishers and product titles against canonical records using exact identifiers first, then controlled aliases and fuzzy comparison. Low-confidence matches require human review and must not silently overwrite historical evidence.

## Temporal inventory

When a tracked asset attribute changes, close the current version and insert a new version in one transaction. Validity periods for one asset cannot overlap. Historical reporting should use the version that contains the requested timestamp.

## License position

Effective position is entitlement quantity minus consumption under the relevant license metric. Shortfall exposure and reclaim value are estimates until commercial terms, bundling, downgrade/upgrade rights, and contract evidence are reviewed.

## Optimization

Prioritize unused installations, duplicate products, shelfware, and approaching renewals. Recommendations remain advisory: a human owner approves uninstall, procurement, and contract actions.
